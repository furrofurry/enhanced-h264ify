😌 Relax mode is on

Scroll down to reach settings.

  • Prevents YouTube from selecting 60fps streams. Useful if you want lower CPU/GPU usage, cooler device temps, better battery life, or smoother playback on weaker hardware/network conditions.

  • Prevents YouTube from selecting H.264/AVC video streams. Normally you’d leave this OFF on older hardware (since H.264 is widely hardware-accelerated). Turn it ON only if you specifically want to force newer codecs instead.

  • Prevents YouTube from selecting VP8 video streams. Keep OFF if you want broader compatibility fallback; turn ON if VP8 performs poorly on your setup.

  • Prevents YouTube from selecting VP9 video streams. Turn ON if VP9 causes stutter/high power draw on your device, or keep OFF if your hardware decodes VP9 efficiently.

  • Prevents YouTube from selecting AV1 video streams. AV1 is efficient for bandwidth but can be heavy on devices without AV1 hardware decoding. Turn ON if AV1 playback is unstable or too CPU-intensive.

  • Prevents YouTube from selecting Opus audio streams. Mostly useful for troubleshooting compatibility or forcing alternate audio container/codec paths.

  • Prevents YouTube from selecting AAC/MP4A audio streams. Use only if you want to avoid AAC specifically (e.g., testing/compatibility scenarios).

  • Disables YouTube’s loudness adjustment/tweaking. With this enabled, audio is played closer to the creator’s original mastered loudness and dynamics (instead of YouTube leveling perceived volume between videos).

  • Hides distracting recommendation panels/areas and replaces the home feed grid with a calmer prompt. Intended for users who want to reduce “infinite scroll” behavior and use YouTube more intentionally.
